## Challenge

The previous WordPress-based website had issues with responsiveness and an unstructured presentation of services. Different services were displayed without a clear organization, which could make it difficult for visitors to understand the available options and find the right path.

## Solution

A new personal website was developed with a focus on better service organization and user experience. Services were structured into clear categories, allowing visitors to quickly identify the solution they need.

The new website includes business coaching services, consultation booking, photography equipment rental, and a direct contact form. The redesigned experience presents the personal brand in a more professional and organized way.

## Development

The website was built using Django and Tailwind CSS, providing a modern responsive interface along with a scalable structure for managing and expanding services.

## Result

The new website provides a clearer service structure, improves user navigation, and helps visitors better understand available services and how to connect with Reza Piri.
