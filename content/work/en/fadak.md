# Fadak Brick Factory

**An industrial website and interactive factory experience for Fadak, a brick manufacturer in Iran.**

Fadak's digital presence was designed to introduce the factory, its products, and its manufacturing capabilities through a dedicated website and an interactive factory experience.

The project combines industrial presentation with practical business communication, helping visitors understand the company, explore its products, and get a closer look at its production environment.

![Fadak Brick Factory website and interactive factory experience](/images/work/fadak/desktop.png)

## The Challenge

An industrial website needs to do more than present a company and list its products. It should communicate production capabilities, establish credibility, and help potential customers understand what the manufacturer can offer.

For a brick factory, the production environment is an important part of that story. Conventional website pages alone may not fully convey the scale of the facility and the processes behind the final product.

The project therefore focused on building a stronger digital presence for Fadak, combining its corporate website with a separate interactive factory experience.

## The Solution

The project consists of two complementary digital experiences.

### Corporate Website

The main website introduces Fadak and provides a digital foundation for presenting its products, manufacturing activities, and business information.

Key areas of the project include:

- Corporate and industrial presentation
- Product and catalog content
- Search engine optimization foundations
- Structured website content
- A foundation for ongoing content management and publishing
- A digital presence aligned with the factory's identity

### Interactive Factory Experience

**[factory.fadakajor.com](https://factory.fadakajor.com/)** is a separate experience designed to bring visitors closer to the factory and its production environment.

The experience uses panoramic factory scenes and production footage to present different areas of the facility in a more immersive format.

Its content includes selected views of the factory environment, production stages, and industrial equipment, offering an alternative to conventional factory photography.

## Industrial Presentation

The project approaches the website as a business communication tool rather than just a visual showcase.

The corporate website communicates the company's identity and products, while the interactive experience provides additional context about the physical facility and its manufacturing environment.

Together, these two parts create a more complete digital introduction to the factory.

## Technology & Implementation

- **Website:** Fadak corporate website
- **Interactive Experience:** Custom panoramic factory tour
- **Media:** 360° panoramic photography and production footage
- **SEO:** Technical SEO foundations and structured content
- **Deployment:** Separate subdomain for the factory experience

## Result

Fadak has a corporate digital presence supported by a dedicated interactive factory experience.

The project demonstrates how industrial businesses can combine product information, company presentation, and immersive media to communicate their capabilities more effectively online.
