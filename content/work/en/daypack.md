## How Daypack started

I’d kept track of my tasks and day-to-day life in to-do apps since high school, both as a reminder and as a record. I used Focus To-Do for about four years and TickTick for less than a year. I lost my records in both. After trying a few more apps, I decided to build one for myself.

## What I wanted to build

I wanted a free app where I could keep my routines and bigger goals in one place and plan each day around whatever came up. I wanted my records to stay on my own device and to be able to save a separate backup.

I also wanted to keep recurring habits separate from ordinary tasks, organize lists into folders, keep a wishlist, and break bigger tasks into smaller steps.
