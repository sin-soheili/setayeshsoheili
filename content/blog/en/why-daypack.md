One Friday I finally had time for something I’d wanted to do for days: sit down and build a to-do app.

I’d been writing almost everything down in to-do apps since high school. Some things went in so I wouldn’t forget them. Others went in because I wanted a record of them. I liked having a routine and a few bigger goals, then planning each day around whatever came up.

## When my records disappeared

I used Focus To-Do for about four years and TickTick for less than a year. I lost my records once in each app. Imagine keeping notes on your tasks and ordinary days since high school, then opening the app one day and finding them gone. That was hard for me because so many of those notes were about days I couldn’t go back and record again.

## What I was looking for

I tried a few more apps. I wanted something completely free that would keep my data on my own device and let me make a backup. I wanted to see recurring habits separately from ordinary tasks, organize lists in folders, keep a wishlist for later, and break bigger tasks down into smaller steps.

After all that trying, I thought I’d build it myself. I still don’t know how far I’ll take it. For now, this is the idea I sat down to work on that Friday.
